# HBS Taxonomy r010

## Group 1: Basic Physical & Skin Characteristics
### 1. Gender
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Primary gender classification for character creation

#### Gender Options

1. **Identify as male**  
  - **Visual**: Masculine-presenting gender identity.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common across all global populations.

2. **Identify as female**  
  - **Visual**: Feminine-presenting gender identity.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common across all global populations.

---

### 2. Visual Heritage
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Observable ancestry category based on physical traits

#### Visual Heritage Options

1. **European Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Lighter skin, varied eye and hair colors, narrow facial features.  
  - **As Seen In**: Populations across Europe and European diaspora.

2. **African Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Darker skin tones, broader nose, fuller lips, coiled hair textures.  
  - **As Seen In**: Sub-Saharan Africa, Afro-Caribbean, and African-American populations.

3. **East Asian Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Straight black hair, epicanthic eye folds, light to medium skin tones.  
  - **As Seen In**: Chinese, Korean, Japanese populations.

4. **South Asian Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Medium to dark skin tones, dark hair, diverse nose and eye shapes.  
  - **As Seen In**: Indian subcontinent including India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka.

5. **Southeast Asian Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Medium-toned skin, rounder face, dark straight hair.  
  - **As Seen In**: Filipino, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian populations.

6. **Native American Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Medium to light-brown skin, straight dark hair, high cheekbones.  
  - **As Seen In**: Indigenous peoples of North, Central, and South America.

7. **Latino Heritage**  
  - **Visual**: Mixed ancestry with a wide range of features from European, Indigenous, and African heritage.  
  - **As Seen In**: Latin American populations, including Central and South America.

### 3. Age
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Developmental life stage estimation based on visual maturity

#### Age Options

1. **Pre-adolescent Child**  
  - **Visual**: Small stature, soft rounded features, high fat-to-muscle ratio.  
  - **As Seen In**: Children typically ages 0–12.

2. **Teenage**  
  - **Visual**: Rapid growth stage, transitional facial features, hormonal changes visible.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adolescents typically ages 13–19.

3. **Youthful Adult**  
  - **Visual**: Fully developed physical features, smooth skin, high vitality.  
  - **As Seen In**: Young adults typically ages 20–30.

4. **Mature Adult**  
  - **Visual**: Subtle signs of aging, increased muscle and bone structure definition.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults typically ages 30–45.

5. **Middle-Aged**  
  - **Visual**: Prominent aging markers, reduced skin elasticity, deepened features.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults typically ages 45–60.

6. **Elderly-Senior**  
  - **Visual**: Wrinkled skin, visible muscle loss, bone prominence.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults 60+.

#### Age Position Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard expression of the age category.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical manifestation of the selected age.

- **Early**  
  - **Visual**: Beginning phase of the age category.  
  - **As Seen In**: Earlier manifestation of the selected age.

- **Mid**  
  - **Visual**: Middle phase of the age category.  
  - **As Seen In**: Average manifestation of the selected age.

- **Late**  
  - **Visual**: Final phase of the age category.  
  - **As Seen In**: Later manifestation of the selected age.

### 4. Build
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Overall body shape and mass distribution

#### Build Options

1. **Slender**  
  - **Visual**: Narrow frame with minimal body mass.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common across all populations, typically with lower body fat percentage.

2. **Rectangle**  
  - **Visual**: Straight vertical silhouette with minimal waist definition.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common among both male and female bodies.

3. **Inverted-Triangle**  
  - **Visual**: Broad shoulders tapering to a narrower waist and hips.  
  - **As Seen In**: Often seen in mesomorphic male builds.

4. **Pear**  
  - **Visual**: Narrow upper body with broader hips and thighs.  
  - **As Seen In**: Often seen in endomorphic female builds.

5. **Hourglass**  
  - **Visual**: Balanced bust and hip width with a narrow waist.  
  - **As Seen In**: Frequently idealized in fashion and fitness models.

6. **Apple**  
  - **Visual**: Rounded midsection with less waist definition.  
  - **As Seen In**: Seen across all genders, especially in middle-aged individuals.

7. **Curvy**  
  - **Visual**: Rounded contours with emphasis on bust, hips, and thighs.  
  - **As Seen In**: Often seen in fuller female body types.

8. **V-Shaped**  
  - **Visual**: Broad upper torso narrowing to the waist.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common in athletic males.

9. **Athletic**  
  - **Visual**: Muscular build with defined structure and minimal fat.  
  - **As Seen In**: Seen in individuals engaged in regular training or sports.

10. **Muscular**  
  - **Visual**: High muscle mass, prominent definition.  
  - **As Seen In**: Bodybuilders, athletes.

11. **Stocky**  
  - **Visual**: Shorter, broad body with thick limbs.  
  - **As Seen In**: Often seen in power athletes.

12. **Heavyset**  
  - **Visual**: Wide, solid frame with higher fat mass.  
  - **As Seen In**: Common in endomorphic types.

13. **Lean**  
  - **Visual**: Thin but toned, low fat with some muscle definition.  
  - **As Seen In**: Endurance athletes, dancers.

#### Build Intensity Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard expression of the build characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical manifestation of the selected build.

- **Slightly**  
  - **Visual**: Mild expression of the build characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Subtle manifestation of the selected build.

- **Moderately**  
  - **Visual**: Clear expression of the build characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Noticeable manifestation of the selected build.

- **Notably**  
  - **Visual**: Strong expression of the build characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Prominent manifestation of the selected build.

- **Extremely**  
  - **Visual**: Maximum expression of the build characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Extreme manifestation of the selected build.

### 5. Height
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Vertical stature relative to gender and heritage norms

#### Height Options

1. **Very Short**  
  - **Visual**: Noticeably below average height for gender and heritage.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults below the 5th percentile of regional height charts.

2. **Short**  
  - **Visual**: Below average stature with proportional limbs.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults in the 5th–25th percentile range.

3. **Average**  
  - **Visual**: Median height range relative to gender and heritage.  
  - **As Seen In**: Most adult populations globally.

4. **Tall**  
  - **Visual**: Above average height with longer limbs.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults in the 75th–95th percentile.

5. **Very Tall**  
  - **Visual**: Exceptional height beyond typical regional averages.  
  - **As Seen In**: Adults above the 95th percentile in global populations.

### 6. Skin Tone
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Observable pigmentation range independent of lighting or styling

#### Skin Tone Options

1. **Very Fair**  
   - **Visual**: Extremely pale skin, often with visible pink or blue undertones.  
   - **As Seen In**: Common among Northern European populations and individuals with albinism.

2. **Fair**  
   - **Visual**: Light skin with subtle pink, yellow, or neutral undertones.  
   - **As Seen In**: Often seen in Central European and East Asian heritage groups.

3. **Light**  
   - **Visual**: Beige-toned skin with golden or neutral undertones.  
   - **As Seen In**: Typical among Southern European, Middle Eastern, and mixed-heritage individuals.

4. **Medium**  
   - **Visual**: Balanced brown skin with no dominant undertone.  
   - **As Seen In**: Common across Southeast Asian, Latino, and Native American populations.

5. **Tan**  
   - **Visual**: Warm brown skin with sun-reactive pigmentation.  
   - **As Seen In**: Frequently seen in Mediterranean and South American descent.

6. **Deep**  
   - **Visual**: Dark brown skin with higher melanin density.  
   - **As Seen In**: Typically found in Sub-Saharan African, Afro-Caribbean, and South Indian heritage.

7. **Very Deep**  
   - **Visual**: Near-black skin, sometimes with red, blue, or violet undertones.  
   - **As Seen In**: Common among Central African and Nilotic populations, and some Aboriginal Australian lineages.

#### Skin Tone Intensity Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard tone of the skin color.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical expression of the selected skin tone.

- **Light**  
  - **Visual**: Lighter variation of the skin tone.  
  - **As Seen In**: Paler expression of the selected skin tone.

- **Rich**  
  - **Visual**: More saturated variation of the skin tone.  
  - **As Seen In**: Vibrant expression of the selected skin tone.

- **Deep**  
  - **Visual**: Darker variation of the skin tone.  
  - **As Seen In**: More intense expression of the selected skin tone.

### 7. Skin Texture
**Status:** Essential
**Description:** Surface quality and condition of skin that affects visual appearance and perceived age

#### Skin Texture Options

1. **Fine-Pored**  
  - **Visual**: Smooth with minimally visible pores and even surface.  
  - **As Seen In**: Naturally youthful or well-maintained skin.

2. **Normal Textured**  
  - **Visual**: Balanced texture with visible pores and natural variations.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical healthy adult skin.

3. **Weather-Worn**  
  - **Visual**: Slightly roughened texture from environmental exposure.  
  - **As Seen In**: Outdoor workers, sailors, farmers.

4. **Line-Detailed**  
  - **Visual**: Natural fine lines that add character without appearing aged.  
  - **As Seen In**: Expressive faces, mature adults.

5. **Textured Mature**  
  - **Visual**: Visible pores, fine wrinkles, and natural aging patterns.  
  - **As Seen In**: Middle-aged to elderly individuals.

6. **Scarred Character**  
  - **Visual**: Distinctive marking patterns that add uniqueness.  
  - **As Seen In**: Various life experiences, certain professions.

#### Skin Texture Visibility Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard visibility of the skin texture.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical manifestation of the selected texture.

- **Subtle**  
  - **Visual**: Minimally noticeable skin texture characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Understated manifestation of the selected texture.

- **Noticeable**  
  - **Visual**: Clearly visible skin texture characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Apparent manifestation of the selected texture.

- **Prominent**  
  - **Visual**: Highly visible skin texture characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Striking manifestation of the selected texture.

# HBS Taxonomy r010

## Group 2: Head & Face Structure
### 8. Head Shape
**Status:** Essential parameter
**Description:** Overall cranial structure that forms the foundation of facial appearance

#### Head Shape Options

1. **Oval**  
   - **Visual**: Slightly elongated curved form with balanced proportions.  
   - **As Seen In**: Classical artistic representations, considered universally balanced.

2. **Round**  
   - **Visual**: Circular shape with equal width and height, softer angles.  
   - **As Seen In**: Certain East Asian populations, youthful appearances.

3. **Square**  
   - **Visual**: Angular with similar width at forehead and jaw, strong lines.  
   - **As Seen In**: Classic masculine ideal, strong-featured individuals.

4. **Rectangular**  
  - **Visual**: Elongated square shape with vertical emphasis.  
  - **As Seen In**: Certain European populations, longer facial structures.

5. **Heart**  
   - **Visual**: Wider at forehead tapering to a narrower chin point.  
   - **As Seen In**: Classic feminine ideal, certain youthful faces.

6. **Diamond**  
   - **Visual**: Narrow forehead widening at cheekbones then tapering to chin.  
   - **As Seen In**: Fashion models, distinctive angular appearances.

7. **Triangle**  
   - **Visual**: Narrow forehead widening significantly at jawline.  
   - **As Seen In**: Certain body types, faces with prominent lower features.

8. **Inverted-Triangle**  
   - **Visual**: Wide forehead narrowing significantly toward chin.  
   - **As Seen In**: Certain athletic builds, faces with prominent upper features.

9. **Long**  
   - **Visual**: Vertically extended with narrow proportions throughout.  
   - **As Seen In**: Certain European and African populations.

10. **Baseball**  
   - **Visual**: Shortened oval with slight angularity at sides.  
   - **As Seen In**: Certain East Asian and Native American populations.

#### Alignment Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard alignment of the head shape.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical expression of the selected shape.

- **Slightly**  
  - **Visual**: Mild expression of the head shape characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Subtle manifestation of the shape.

- **Classically**  
  - **Visual**: Archetypal expression of the head shape characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Textbook example of the shape.

- **Perfectly**  
  - **Visual**: Ideal expression of the head shape characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Flawless manifestation of the shape.

#### Definition Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard definition of head shape contours.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical delineation of head shape.

- **Softly**  
  - **Visual**: Gentle transitions between head shape contours.  
  - **As Seen In**: Subtle delineation of head shape.

- **Moderately**  
  - **Visual**: Clear but not pronounced head shape contours.  
  - **As Seen In**: Balanced delineation of head shape.

- **Sharply**  
  - **Visual**: Distinct and pronounced head shape contours.  
  - **As Seen In**: Strong delineation of head shape.

#### Prominence Modifiers

- **None** (default)  
  - **Visual**: Standard prominence of head shape characteristics.  
  - **As Seen In**: Typical manifestation of head shape.

- **Subtle**  
  - **Visual**: Minimal expression of distinctive head shape features.  
  - **As Seen In**: Understated head shape characteristics.

- **Pronounced**  
  - **Visual**: Strong expression of distinctive head shape features.  
  - **As Seen In**: Emphasized head shape characteristics.
